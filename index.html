Before You Write

What to include

To help us resolve your issue faster, please include the information below.

  • App version
  • Device model
  • iOS or iPadOS version
  • Short description of the issue
  • Screenshots, if relevant
Response Time

How we handle requests

We aim to reply within 2 business days. Feature requests are reviewed regularly, but we cannot guarantee implementation dates.

FAQ

Common support topics

  • Invoice issue: tell us what you were trying to create or export and what went wrong.
  • Bug report: tell us what you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Data deletion request: email support and describe what data you want removed.
  • Privacy question: review our Privacy Policy or contact support.
